Indicating connections between nodes in the same column — 12 ways

Each mock shows a single column of project bars carrying 9 same-column connections (drawn in full). Project bars have their left and right edges partly occupied by the cross-column ribbon bands (theme colours left, skill colours right) — shown as the small coloured ticks on each bar. So a same-column link has to live in the outer channel, the row gaps, a side panel, or use a no-line encoding. The green note on each card says how it copes with the occupied sides.

Rough verdict for 9 links on projects: line-routing methods (arcs, lanes, traces, ribbons) get crowded fast and fight the side bands — best kept to a reserved outer channel and few links. For a full 9, the non-routing encodings scale best: adjacency matrix (4), numbered partners (5), and hover-reveal (9) stay legible at any count and don't touch the occupied edges. A good combo: matrix or numbers for the always-on overview + hover-reveal for detail.
